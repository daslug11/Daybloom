# Daybloom design plan

This is the single prioritized plan from design Phase 1. It combines:

- an Impeccable `critique` and `audit` of all 17 surfaces, one isolated reviewer each, in [`docs/design/reviews/`](docs/design/reviews/);
- the `improve-animations` motion audit ([`plans/README.md`](plans/README.md), plans 001–006);
- the `remove-animations` list ([`plans/REMOVALS.md`](plans/REMOVALS.md)).

It's measured against the baseline in [`docs/design/BASELINE.md`](docs/design/BASELINE.md) and governed by [`PRODUCT.md`](PRODUCT.md) and [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md).

**How the reviews were run.**
- Each reviewer wrote its design critique from the screenshots, the source and the live app **before** opening the detector results.
- Then it did the technical audit with those results.
- They used the seeded demo account and blocked or avoided writes. No AI was called.

**Questions skipped.** The critique flow normally ends by asking the owner questions. The owner asked for an uninterrupted run, so the questions were skipped and their answers taken from DESIGN.md's confirmed direction.

## Cross-cutting issues: fix once, fixes everywhere

These recur on many pages. Each is fixed once in a shared component or token, in the phase shown. The per-page rows they cause point back here.

| # | Sev | Issue | Where it shows | Fix | Fix with | Phase |

## How the work is phased

| Phase | Theme | What it fixes from this plan |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Foundations | **X1–X8 and X10 first** (the three P0 bugs and the shared dialog/toast/focus fixes), then type scale, spacing, the neutral colour system, contrast and extracting shared components. Motion plan **001** (tokens) also lands here, because the new components use it. |
| 3 | Remove the junk | Everything tagged `distill`, `quieter` or `remove-animations`, plus [`plans/REMOVALS.md`](plans/REMOVALS.md) and motion plans **002–003**. |
| 4 | Page by page | Everything tagged `clarify`, `onboard`, `polish`, `bolder`, `delight` or `overdrive` (voice HUD only), **X9**, and every P1 not already handled. |
| 5 | Motion | Motion plans **004–006**, then `review-animations` and `animation-performance`. |
| 6 | Mobile | Everything tagged `mobile-native` or `adapt`, plus **X11**. |
| 7 | Break it, then harden it | Remaining `harden` P2/P3 items, **X12**, then `break-ui` worst cases with browser tests. |
| 8 | Performance | `optimize` items and bundle/load work. |
| 9 | Final pass | `polish` everywhere; re-run critique, audit, the detector and review-animations; fix anything P0–P2 still open. |

**Assignment rules:** every P0 is fixed in Phase 2. A P1 whose natural command belongs to a later phase is pulled forward into Phase 4 at the latest.

**Detector false positives** (verified by the reviewers, not to be "fixed"):
- "AI colour palette" and "dark glow" hits on the **Atlas orb's cyan**: cyan is reserved for Atlas. Its glow will still become a neutral shadow under the no-glows direction.
- "Overused font": one sans family (Inter) is a documented choice.
- "Repeating stripes": the stripes mark locked calendar events; they carry meaning.

**Two findings the owner should know about now:**
- **The login page puts any `?error=` text verbatim in its error box.** It isn't an injection hole, but it could be used for phishing; fixed in Phase 2 (Auth).
- **The Calendars help says secret calendar links stay "in this browser",** but they are saved to the account. This is a privacy claim the app doesn't keep. Fixed in Phase 2 (copy correction).
